# F&B Finishes - Quick Reference

## Main Finishes:

**Dead Flat** - Ultra-matte, scrubbable, works on walls/trim/ceilings/doors
- Use for: Envelope effect (one color everything), color-drenching, modern sophistication
- Benefit: Seamless, no sheen variation

**Estate/Modern Emulsion** - Flat finish for walls
- Estate = Traditional homes, Modern = Contemporary
- Use for: Standard wall finish

**Estate/Modern Eggshell** - Low-sheen for walls/trim/cabinets
- Washable, durable
- Use for: Kitchens, bathrooms, high-traffic, cabinets

**Full Gloss** - High sheen for trim/doors
- Maximum durability
- Use for: Traditional high-contrast trim

---

## Strategies:

**Color-Drenching (Envelope Effect):**
- Dead Flat on walls, trim, ceiling (all same color)
- Creates seamless, sophisticated look

**Double-Drenching:**
- 2+ related colors in same family on different surfaces
- Example: Walls in Color A, trim in Color B (both from same tonal family)
- Both in Dead Flat or matching sheen

**Traditional:**
- Walls: Emulsion
- Trim: Eggshell or Full Gloss (contrast)
- Ceiling: Emulsion

---

## Quick Rules:

Walls → Emulsion or Dead Flat  
Trim/Doors → Dead Flat, Eggshell, or Full Gloss  
Ceilings → Emulsion or Dead Flat (never Eggshell/Gloss)  
Cabinets → Eggshell (durable) or Dead Flat (modern)

**Dead Flat is the modern, sophisticated choice for color-drenching.**
